Methodology

How VeriSource scores a source

Every verdict fuses three independent signals with dynamic weights — when one channel has weak evidence (unknown domain, no text, no search hits), its weight is reduced and redistributed to the others.

Source reputation 0.35

A curated database of known outlets (scores, political bias, source type) from Media Bias/Fact Check, Ad Fontes & AllSides, with .gov/.edu TLD fallbacks.

Content analysis 0.40

An LLM scores factuality, objectivity, transparency & sensationalism-restraint, and extracts the article's load-bearing claims and red flags.

Cross-reference 0.25

Each claim is searched on the open web (excluding the original domain); independent hits are labeled supports / contradicts and weighted by their own reputation.
